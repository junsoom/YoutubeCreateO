# The Official Loch Ness Monster Sightings Register
## Loch Ness Sightings 1986 -1999
Just a word on these sightings. The Official Loch Ness Monster Fan Club started this register in 1996 and this register continues to collect data to this day.
The sightings reported between 1985 (when Henry Bauer's list concluded) and 1995 have been collected from a number of sources - direct reports, newspaper articles, Rip Hepple's Nessletters, the [Loch Ness Mystery Blogspot](http://lochnessmystery.blogspot.co.uk/) (where the Nessletters are now digitised) and with the help of Dr Charles Paxton of St Andrews University
**1999**
**Total for 1999 - 4**
14.9.99
It was around 11.30 a.m. when a couple touring the area spotted in Urquhart Bay what they first thought to be a car tyre on the surface of the loch. It appeared to be about three feet long and one foot tall at its highest point. The object then moved against the waves formation in the area creating a wake as it did so.
The couple saw some kind of water disturbance about three feet behind the black coloured object, which appeared to rise slightly out of the water before submerging.
5.7.99
A couple on holiday from Huntly witnessed a creature in the middle of the loch at 9am.  They watched as it moved up the loch for a period of around 20 minutes close to the Loch Ness Caravan and Camping Park. An experienced whale watcher, the man said that he had never before seen anything like it.
30.3.99
A group of 6-7 people saw a head and neck rise from the water 200 yards from the south shore of the loch.  The watched as a black shape rose and then disappeared only to reappear a few minutes later.  The was witnessed from a lay by opposite Urquhart castle.  One witnessed described the head as being about 18 inches in height.
22.2.99
For the first time in since June 1963, Nessie was spotted out of the water on the shores of Loch Ness by an American visitor to the area.  This unique sighting took place at 8.30 in the evening on the beach between Dores and Foyers where the creature, said to be between 10-15 metres in length with a long neck, scurried off into the water as the man approached.

...

They were turning starboard showing us their left side which indicated they were not small but because the surface was disturbed by their movement the rear was not clear enough to gauge the length. The front shape was clear enough to see it was not long but more of a crocodile shape with a left forward fin and a broad back and a tail which must have provided the power. My boat was 72ft and the broken water ellipse was at least 70 – 100 yards long.
We observed them for at least 10 minutes in which time they looked as if they were chasing their tails and never stopped or dived. Looking back, it could be they were corralling the salmon which was plentiful at the time or it was a breeding courtship"
Detailing the actual sighting:-
Our height of eye from whaleback was at least 15ft and distance from the nearest edge of disturbed water was 20-25ft. Weather that day was breath calm and the creatures never stopped moving or diving. We stayed at least 10 minutes observing them and they were still active when we decided to leave. Our estimate of the creatures was about 20 – 30ft"
