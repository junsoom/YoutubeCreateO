_**Close Encounters of the Third Kind**_ is a 1977 American [science fiction drama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_fiction_drama "Science fiction drama") film written and directed by [Steven Spielberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Spielberg "Steven Spielberg") , starring [Richard Dreyfuss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dreyfuss "Richard Dreyfuss") , [Melinda Dillon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melinda_Dillon "Melinda Dillon") , [Teri
Garr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teri_Garr "Teri Garr") , [Bob Balaban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Balaban "Bob Balaban") , [Cary Guffey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cary_Guffey "Cary Guffey") , and [François Truffaut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fran%C3%A7ois_Truffaut "François Truffaut") .
The film depicts the interconnected stories of Roy Neary (Dreyfuss), an [everyday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everyman "Everyman") [blue-collar worker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue-collar_worker "Blue-collar worker") in [Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana "Indiana") whose life changes after an encounter with an [unidentified flying object](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unidentified_flying_object "Unidentified flying object") (UFO); Jillian Guiler (Dillon), a single mother

# Release and reception
## Critical reception
The website's consensus reads: " _Close Encounters of the Third Kind_ is deeply humane sci-fi exploring male obsession, cosmic mysticism, and music." [[ 66 ]]() On [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic "Metacritic") , the film has a weighted average score of 90 out of 100, based on 10 reviews, indicating "universal acclaim".

# Legacy
[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Close_Encounters_of_the_Third_Kind&action=edit&section=16 "Edit section: Legacy") ]
See also: [Night Skies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Night_Skies "Night Skies")

In 2011, [ABC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Broadcasting_Company "American Broadcasting Company") aired a primetime special, _Best in Film: The Greatest Movies of Our Time_ , that counted down the best films chosen by fans based on results of a poll conducted by ABC and [_People_ magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People_(magazine) "People (magazine)") . _Close Encounters of the Third Kind_ was selected as the #5 Best Sci-Fi Film.
[[ 110 ]]() _The Guardian_ also selected the film as the 11th best Sci-Fi and fantasy film of all time. [[ 111 ]]() In 2024, Close Encounters of the Third Kind was included in Rolling Stone's "The 150 Best Science Fiction Movies of All Time" list at #3. [[ 112 ]]()
