The **Bermuda Triangle** , also known as the **Devil's Triangle** , is a loosely defined region in the [North Atlantic Ocean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_Ocean "Atlantic Ocean") , roughly bounded by [Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida "Florida") , [Bermuda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bermuda "Bermuda") , and [Puerto Rico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Rico "Puerto Rico") .
Since the mid-20th century, it has been the focus of an [urban legend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban_legend "Urban legend") suggesting that many [aircraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aircraft "Aircraft") , [ships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ship "Ship") , and people have disappeared there under mysterious circumstances. However, extensive investigations by reputable sources, including the [U.S.
federal government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_government_of_the_United_States "Federal government of the United States") and scientific organizations, have found no evidence of unusual activity, attributing reported incidents to natural phenomena, human error, and misinterpretation. ## Natural explanations
### Violent weather
[](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Atlantic_hurricane_tracks.jpg) Tracks of all known Atlantic hurricanes between 1851 and 2019. Many storms pass through the Bermuda Triangle.
Hurricanes are powerful storms which form in tropical waters and have cost thousands of lives and caused billions of dollars in damage. The sinking of [Francisco de Bobadilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francisco_de_Bobadilla "Francisco de Bobadilla") 's Spanish fleet in 1502 was the first recorded instance of a destructive hurricane. [[ 42 ]](,_1974,_pp._25%E2%80%9328-45) These storms have caused a number of incidents related to the Triangle.

...

### Methane hydrates
Publications by the [USGS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USGS "USGS") describe large stores of undersea hydrates worldwide, including the [Blake Ridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blake_Ridge "Blake Ridge") area, off the coast of the southeastern United States. [[ 50 ]]() However, according to the USGS, no large releases of gas hydrates are believed to have occurred in the Bermuda Triangle for the past 15,000 years. [[ 3 ]]()

## HMS _Atalanta_
The sail training ship HMS _Atalanta_ (originally named HMS _Juno_ ) disappeared with her entire crew after setting sail from the [Royal Naval Dockyard, Bermuda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Naval_Dockyard,_Bermuda "Royal Naval Dockyard, Bermuda") for [Falmouth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falmouth,_Cornwall "Falmouth, Cornwall") , [England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England "England") on 31 January 1880.
